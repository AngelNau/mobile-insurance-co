# General Terms of Mobile Device Insurance (MobiProtect Ltd.)

## Article 1 – Subject of insurance
Only devices (mobile phones and laptops) whose brand and model are listed on a valid insurance policy are insured. Devices not listed on the policy (e.g. tablets, smartwatches, other devices of the policyholder) are not covered. A claim that refers to a non-existent policy number is rejected.

## Article 2 – Insurance period
Cover applies from the start date to the end date stated on the policy. Incidents that occur before the start or after the end of the insurance period are not covered.

## Article 3 – Accidental physical damage
Sudden and accidental physical damage to the device caused by a drop, impact, slip or crushing is covered, including a cracked screen, a cracked casing and a damaged camera.

## Article 4 – Liquid damage
Damage to the device caused by accidental contact with liquid, for example a spilled drink, a drop into water or moisture ingress, is covered.

## Article 5 – Theft and loss
Theft of the device is covered only if it is a burglary or robbery and the policyholder reported the incident to the police. Ordinary theft without signs of a break-in (e.g. from an unlocked vehicle), theft without a police report, and loss, forgetting or misplacing the device are not covered.

## Article 6 – Exclusions
The insurance does not cover: (a) intentional damage to the device by the policyholder or by a third party with their knowledge; (b) wear, ageing and battery degradation; (c) software errors, viruses and data loss; (d) purely cosmetic damage (scratches) that does not affect operation; (e) manufacturing defects covered by the manufacturer's warranty.

## Article 7 – Sum insured and limit
The insurer pays out at most up to the limit stated on the policy, and never more than the actual repair or replacement cost stated by the policyholder.

## Article 8 – Deductible
The deductible (the policyholder's own share) stated on the policy is subtracted from every recognized payout. If the recognized amount is lower than the deductible, nothing is paid out.

## Article 9 – Depreciation
The value of the device is reduced by 20 % for each completed year of the device's age from the purchase date to the date of the incident, but by no more than 60 % in total. The recognized amount may not exceed the depreciated value of the device.

## Article 10 – Assessor review
Before payout, the claim must be reviewed by an assessor if (a) the claimed amount exceeds €800 or (b) the incident occurred less than 30 days after the start of the insurance. In these cases the final decision is made by the assessor.

## Article 11 – Filing a claim
When filing a claim, the policyholder must state the policy number, the device, the date of the incident, a description or type of damage and the claimed amount. An incomplete claim is completed at the insurer's request.

## Article 12 – Premium payment
If the policy is suspended due to an unpaid premium, the insurer does not cover incidents that occur during the suspension.
